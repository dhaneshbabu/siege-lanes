How to play

The goal

Be the last knight standing. Each round is in a fresh courtyard; the first side to win the set number of rounds takes the match.

Controls

Put your left thumb anywhere in the left half of the controls and drag to run. Tap Keg with your right thumb to drop a powder keg where you stand. On a keyboard: arrow keys or WASD to run, Space to drop a keg.

    Blasts

    A keg bursts about two seconds after you drop it, in a cross along the row and the column. Stone stops a blast. A crate stops it too, and breaks. A blast that reaches another keg sets it off at once.

    Your own blasts can get you, so drop a keg and get round a corner. In 2 v 2, your ally's blasts don't hurt you.

    Power-ups

    Some crates hide a power-up. Walk over it to pick it up. Blasts burn power-ups lying on the ground.

      Closing in

      About a minute into each round, stone starts falling from the edges inwards (a red square marks the next spot) until only the middle is left. Then comes the last stand: kegs burn faster and every blast reaches all the way across. A few seconds later the stone starts falling again. If everyone left is caught at the same moment, the round is a draw.

      Tips

      Never drop a keg in a dead end. Count your way out before you drop it. Grab Bigger blast early, it makes trapping rivals much easier.
