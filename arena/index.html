      How to play

      The goal

      Be the last knight standing. Each round is in a fresh courtyard; the first side to win the set number of rounds takes the match.

      Controls

      Put your left thumb anywhere in the left half of the controls and drag to run. Tap Keg with your right thumb to drop a powder keg where you stand. On a keyboard: arrow keys or WASD to run, Space to drop a keg.

        Blasts

        A keg bursts about two seconds after you drop it, in a cross along the row and the column. Stone stops a blast. A crate stops it too, and breaks. A blast that reaches another keg sets it off at once.

        Your own blasts can get you, so drop a keg and get round a corner.

        In 2 v 2, teammates wear two shades of one colour: blue and light blue against red and orange. Your ally's blasts don't hurt you, so the cells their kegs will hit glow in their colour instead of red.

        Power-ups

        About one crate in three hides a power-up: a glowing coin that pops out when the crate breaks. Walk over it to pick it up. Its name shows when you get close. A blast that reaches a power-up on the ground burns it.

          The panel under the courtyard shows what you have: how many kegs you can have down at once (filled ones are ready), how far your blasts reach, and how fast you run.

          Before a keg goes off, the cells it will hit glow red, getting redder as the fuse burns down. Pick up Bigger blast and you'll see your red cross grow.

          Closing in

          About a minute into each round, stone starts falling from the edges inwards (a red square marks the next spot) until only the middle is left. Then comes the last stand: kegs burn faster and every blast reaches all the way across. A few seconds later the stone starts falling again. If everyone left is caught at the same moment, the round is a draw.

          Playing with friends

          One phone hosts: it gets a four-letter code and a QR code to share. Everyone else joins with the code, on the same Wi-Fi or from anywhere. Bots take any empty seats, and the host can tap two knights in the lobby to swap their places (that's how you pick sides for 2 v 2).

          The game runs on the host's phone, so keep its screen on. If a friend's phone locks or loses signal, a bot plays for it until it's back.

          Tips

          Never drop a keg in a dead end. Count your way out before you drop it. Grab Bigger blast early, it makes trapping rivals much easier.
