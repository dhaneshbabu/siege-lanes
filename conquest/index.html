How to play

The goal

Every holding with your banner trains soldiers. Use them to take more holdings. Wipe the other banners off the map to win outright.

Matches last 4 minutes. If time runs out, the side holding the most land wins: a village counts 1, a town 2 and a castle 3. The coloured bar next to the clock shows how the land is split.

Send soldiers

Drag from one of your holdings to any other holding and let go. To send from several of yours at once, drag across them on the way to the target.

Or tap one of yours, then tap the target. Each tap on the target sends another group. Tap empty ground to let go of your selection.

The buttons at the bottom set how much of each garrison marches: a quarter, half, or all of it. The small number under each one is how many soldiers that would send.

Fights

Soldiers that reach a holding that isn't yours fight its garrison. If more arrive than defend it, it's yours, and the survivors become the new garrison. If not, they still kill defenders, so a second wave can finish the job.

Soldiers sent to your own holdings join the garrison there. A red ring means enemy soldiers are marching on that holding.

Holdings

    Grey holdings belong to nobody. They don't train soldiers, but they put up a fight.

    Upgrade

    Tap one of your holdings, then Upgrade. It costs soldiers from that garrison: 12 to turn a village into a town, 25 to turn a town into a castle. Bigger holdings train faster, hold more, defend better and count for more land.

    Tips

    Strike a rival holding right after it sends its soldiers away. Don't empty a holding next to a strong enemy. In 2 v 2, your ally's holdings count as yours for the land race, and you can send soldiers into them.
