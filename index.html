How to play

The goal

Enemies march down the road towards your castle gate. Each one that gets through costs lives (Ogres 2, Siege Giants 5). Lose all 20 and your castle falls. Outlast your opponent.

Build

Towers stand on the stone plots beside the road. Plots light up gold when you can afford a tower; tap one to build. Tap a tower to upgrade or sell it. Selling within 4 seconds of building gives all the gold back.

    Each tower has three levels, and you can tell them apart on the board:

    Attack

    The bar at the bottom sends enemies down your opponent's road. Each send costs gold now and raises your income for the rest of the match. Income pays out every few seconds; the ring next to it shows when.

    Your enemies carry your banner. The strip at the top shows your opponent's road as a line from the forest to their castle, with your units on it, so you can watch them get through or fall. Tap the strip to watch their whole lane; tap Back to return to yours.

    Know your enemy

      Waves and sudden death

      Both players face the same waves at the same moments, and they get tougher over time. Ogres can be sent after a short while and Siege Giants later on. Late in the match sudden death begins: every leak costs double and the waves toughen faster, so someone falls soon after.
